# VIS-TRANSFER-LINKED-WORK-01 — Linked-work eligibility source review

**Status:** Report-only review reconciled under approved contract `VIS-TRANSFER-OWNER-DECISION-RECON-04` (superseding `VIS-TRANSFER-OWNER-DECISION-RECON-03` and `VIS-TRANSFER-SOURCE-RECON-01`). Snapshot: 2026-11-06. No code, schema, policy, database, or patient data changed or accessed.

## Executive finding

The schema can represent service orders/results, prescriptions/dispensing, and invoices/receipts/debt linked to a visit. Domain models expose some state and lifecycle methods, but the reviewed checkout has no Application use cases or Infrastructure persistence adapters for these linked-work areas. Consequently, schema fields are not evidence that the current application can load a complete, current, transactionally consistent eligibility snapshot. Transfer must remain default-deny until that source is implemented and verified.

Under approved owner contract `VIS-TRANSFER-OWNER-DECISION-RECON-04` (carrying forward `VIS-TRANSFER-OWNER-DECISION-RECON-03`), the visit-transfer policy is settled:
- **Visit state and timestamps:** visit status, clinical visit date `ngayKham`, and existing examination timestamps (`thoiGianBatDauKham`, `thoiGianKetThucKham`) are preserved during transfer; a destination queue allocation must not rewrite the clinical visit date or examination timeline.
- **Transfer reason:** a reason is mandatory as a fixed reason code; a short note is optional (code taxonomy, bounded-note validation/length/normalization, and privacy rules remain implementation decisions; clinical reason-for-examination fields must not be reused).
- **Queue date:** transfer date is used as a separate queue date (`queueDate`), distinct from `ngayKham`. The local business date uses `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`; UTC remains for technical/audit metadata. No silent mutation of `ngayKham`.
- **Linked-work preservation:** visit-linked service orders, prescriptions, results, invoices, and financial records stay attached to the same visit; transfer does not cancel or mutate them, alter billing, or create duplicate charges.
- **Safe pending handoff:** pending work can follow only when transfer is clinically and operationally safe and the performing unit and responsibility remain intact; otherwise require completion/handoff or deny transfer.
- **Immutability:** issued prescriptions, confirmed results, and posted financial transactions are immutable; adjustments require a separate authorized, reconciled, audited workflow.
- **Transaction consistency and optimistic concurrency:** enforce transaction consistency, optimistic concurrency, domain invariants, and exception-path tests.
- **Timezone rules:** technical/audit metadata uses UTC; clinic business time uses `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`.

These approved rules must not be mislabeled as unresolved recommendations. What remains is an implementation and evidence gap: because the current application lacks readers and adapters to reliably inspect linked work and prove safe handoff, transfer must remain fail-closed and denied.

## Implementation gaps, technical dependencies, and mandatory fail-closed denial

The linked-work rules enumerated above are user-approved policies under `VIS-TRANSFER-OWNER-DECISION-RECON-04` (carrying forward `RECON-03`), not unresolved recommendations. However, the repository currently lacks the technical capability and schema evidence to prove compliance with these rules at runtime:

1. **Missing linked-work readers:** No Application handlers or Infrastructure persistence adapters exist to query service orders, laboratory results, prescriptions, internal stock-issue vouchers, invoices, receipts, or debt.
2. **Inability to prove safe transfer:** Without transactionally consistent readers capable of resolving performing room/clinician, pharmacy location, and dispensing/finality state, the application cannot prove that pending work is safe to transfer or that responsibility remains intact.
3. **Absence of finality indicators:** Statuses in SQL Server are unconstrained strings, and the schema lacks explicit confirmed-result or posted-receipt markers.

Before transfer can be enabled, an implementation task must build a transactionally consistent source that, for the exact visit, can enumerate **all** service orders and result children; prescriptions and every line; linked dispense vouchers and inventory movements; and every invoice, line, receipt, and debt record. It should include status, `isDeleted`, version, timestamps and relevant ownership/location fields, validate parent-child links, decode statuses without fallback, and expose failures distinctly from “none found.” The service-order query should resolve the performing unit and responsible clinician; prescription/stock reads should resolve dispensing location and issue state. Billing eligibility should resolve all invoices and reconcile source lines/receipts without creating or changing financial artifacts.

Preserve the approved fail-closed requirement: **deny transfer whenever linked-work eligibility cannot be proven**, including a missing repository/handler, empty-shaped fallback, unknown/mismatched status, partial or soft-deleted evidence that prevents reconciliation, unassigned pending work, result/issue/payment evidence without a reliable finality state, multiple/ambiguous invoice results, or dependency/concurrency failure. Unknown or mismatched statuses must fail closed; do not infer eligibility from them. A verified empty linked-work set or a fully identified safe pending handoff may be considered only by a later implementation under the approved contract; this review authorizes neither.

## Decisions that remain open without inferred answers

Under approved contract `VIS-TRANSFER-OWNER-DECISION-RECON-04`, the following decisions remain explicitly open and unresolved; do not infer answers or treat them as approved:

1. **Transfer reason taxonomy and validation rules:** while a transfer reason is mandatory as a fixed reason code and a short note is optional, the allowed reason-code taxonomy, bounded-note validation/length/normalization rules, and privacy/audit storage requirements remain open implementation decisions. Clinical reason fields must not be reused.
2. **Queue sequence namespace, allocation reuse/gap policy, and exact transaction sampling semantics for `queueDate`:** while `queueDate` is settled as a separate transfer date in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` distinct from `ngayKham` (no silent mutation of `ngayKham`), the queue sequence namespace (strictly room/day vs alternative namespaces), sequence number reuse/gap policy for cancelled/deleted allocations, and exact transaction sampling semantics (such as handling transactions that cross midnight during execution) remain open.
3. **Dedicated immutable transfer history versus audit-only (or both):** whether transfer history is stored in a dedicated immutable table, audit-only logging, or both.
4. **Idempotent replay semantics:** client operation key namespace, retention duration, recovery across restarts/sessions, payload equality fingerprinting, and exact failure response codes (including handling a key reused with a different payload).
5. **Persistence schema shape and migration authorization:** detailed persistence schema and DDL remain unapproved proposals; no executable migration is authorized.

Visit status, `ngayKham`, and examination timestamps are settled as preserved; they are not an open decision. No DDL or schema migrations are approved or authorized. SEC-01 remains OPEN and transfer workflows remain default-deny.
